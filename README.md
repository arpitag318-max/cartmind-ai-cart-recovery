# CARTMIND — AI-Powered Cart Recovery & Product Intelligence

## Overview
CARTMIND is an AI-powered cart recovery and product intelligence project designed to make recovery recommendations more context-aware by combining customer behaviour, purchase history and product information.

## Problem Statement
Generic cart recovery messages often overlook differences in customer intent, shopping behaviour and product preferences. CARTMIND explores how AI-driven workflows can support more contextual recovery decisions.

## Key Components
- **Customer Behaviour Agent:** Analyses customer behaviour signals.
- **Recovery Decision Agent:** Supports the selection of recovery strategies.
- **Product Intelligence:** Uses product information to support relevant recommendations.
- **Cart Recovery Orchestrator:** Coordinates the different workflow components.
- **Recovery Messaging:** Structures contextual cart recovery messages.

## Technology Stack
- Workflow Automation: n8n
- Vector Database: Pinecone
- Embeddings: BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5
- AI and LLM-based workflow components

## Project Architecture
Customer and product information → Behaviour analysis → Recovery decision → Product intelligence → Orchestrated recovery workflow.

## Project Status
Prototype / workflow development. Refer to the documented workflows for implemented components and current capabilities.

## Objective
Explore how multi-agent AI workflows can make cart recovery more relevant, contextual and data-informed.
